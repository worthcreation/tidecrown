# Tidecrown history (newest first)

- Build 13 (9 Oct 2026): XP bursts, the Shadow Forest cluster, and Swimming. The Shadow Forest is a collection of seven islands, each its own kind: the Landing (grove, with the rowboat, flint rock and forage), the Maze and the Lesser Maze (walls of black trees, mazes generated per island), the Fallow and the Hollow's Isle (grey bubbling ground, dead trees, most of the real Hollowmaws; the Hollow lies on its own isle to the north), the Clearing (serene, fireflies, its own light) and the Far Grove. Two plank bridges (Landing to Maze, Maze to Fallow); every other crossing is a swim. Each island has a fishing spot and forage, and its name shows when you first set foot on it. Map tab: every island drawn with the bridges and your position, and a warp button to each, for testing until travel is a system. You tab: a Runescape-shaped paperdoll with ten gear slots (new: back, neck, scarf, legs, ring, feet), the character drawn, name, origin, total level, essence; gear moved out of the Pack tab. Swimming reworked: walking into water sinks you gradually through the shallows, and where it gets deep the walk becomes a swim; the swimmer lies prone on the water; only the submerged part of the body changes colour (tinted through an offscreen canvas so the water never boxes the scenery), a transparent sheet flows over the body at the waterline, and the swell drifts the swimmer; swimming works in the Shadow cluster between its islands; the swim from the Key to the dark islands is much longer, and they rise on the horizon as you go. Shadow trees can be chopped (always ready, no regrowth, cuts are saved); the middle tree of a maze wall holds it, cut that and the wall opens for walking and routing; routing is a walkability grid over the whole island, so any spot reaches any spot and the Hollow's zone no longer traps you. The Shadow shore has two bubbling spots and one black-water spot to fish, a rock with flint for anyone who arrives without a hatchet, and forage the dark water leaves (it comes back after a minute and a half). Swimmer: the submerged part of the body is tinted dark with a wavering waterline; stop stroking and you sink, the water climbs you and breath goes faster; a slide (swipe) strokes in that direction with the slide's speed setting the push. Swimming: tap the water to wade in, tap in rhythm with the ring to stroke toward where you tapped; strokes on the beat cost no breath and build an In the groove streak; breath runs down and the shore takes you back when it is gone; lantern jellies drift and sting (stun, breath, knockback); floating shells, kelp, bait and bottles are yours if you swim through them; swim far enough east and the dark islands take you in. Swimming subskill under Exploration with achievements and unlocks. Shadow Forest: a placeholder rowboat at the end of the pier rows to a cluster of dark islands (travel proper waits for Sailing). The big island is a maze of dark trees across it, always dim, with a small circle of light around you. Mossbacks wander the maze and follow once fed a cooked fish; Hollowmaws stand as dead trees, six of them among a dozen dead trees that look exactly the same (some creak in the wind to keep you guessing), in corridors as well as dead ends; the real ones creak, then lunge when you come close; dead trees and Hollowmaws share one drawing in the forest's own dark palette, each shaped by its seed (height, lean, broken branches, hanging bark), and a real one shows only a seam down the chest and, rarely, the faintest warm fleck in it; the maze is sized so a walkable ring of shore runs all the way round it; Moonlappers drink at the north shore at night and vanish when approached; the Hollow lies across the north end, breathing. Journal entries for all of it. Saves remember which island you are on. XP bursts, first pass for feel. Base XP cut to about 40% everywhere. Bursts are named gold showers with their XP under them (burst() and streak() in 14), found by doing and listed on the subskill page once earned. Fishing: Streak of hooks (×3, ×5, then every 5), Clean hook mark on a tight hook. Woodcutting: Streak of clean hits across chops, Clean log for a log with no misses. Cooking: Golden run streak of golden pans. Foraging: Last second for a grab while the next wave is already breaking. Bursts queue so they do not pile up.
- Build 12 (9 Oct 2026): full screen on phones. Web app manifest (display fullscreen, portrait), hand-drawn app icon (icon.svg plus PNG renders for iPhone), Apple web-app meta tags. On Android the first tap on the canvas requests fullscreen; iPhone Safari has no fullscreen API, so a one-time toast after 75 s suggests Add to Home Screen, which launches full screen with the status bar translucent.
- Build 11 (9 Oct 2026): a day is 24 real minutes with two tides, so the tide cycles every 12 and the water is always moving. Waves near you aim at a sparkling surface six times in ten, and a wave that hits a surface already holding salt rarely leaves more.
- Build 10 (9 Oct 2026): timing pass toward Runescape scale. Fires: 90 s from lighting (bonfire 180), cap 5 min (8), driftwood +45 s, kelp +12 s, charcoal +40 s, wobblewood +90 s (+120 at Firemaking 3); cooking burns fuel 1.5x; examine reports seconds under 90 s. Trees regrow in 3 min. Fishing spots live 2 to 5 min and keep moving whether or not you are fishing; a spot that fades under your line ends the cast. Day and night now run on real time at 48 min per day, two tides a day. Salt: a wave that breaks on rock or piling leaves salt about half the time, and it stays until the next wave hits that surface.
- Build 9 (9 Oct 2026): wobble trees. A chopped tree drops a branch and loses most of its canopy, which grows back over eight real minutes (S.treeCut); it can be chopped again at about two-thirds grown; leaves fall at the cut. Chop cue: a small inner circle pulses in and out on the trunk while the beat window is open, no colour change.
- Build 8 (9 Oct 2026): wave sizes. About half of waves are small and only break on the shore, rocks and pier, carrying one thing at most and usually nothing. A third are medium. The rare big ones (one in seven) run far up the beach, show a thicker swell line and foam, and carry one to three things. Reach also rises a little with the tide.
- Build 7 (9 Oct 2026): salt only from waves, fishing spots roam. Rocks and pilings go wet with a splash the moment the foam reaches them and sparkle as it pulls back past them; no timers. Fishing spots (shallow and wild water) fade in, live one to two minutes, fade out and bubble up somewhere else; a spot waits while you are fishing it; the glowing pool stays put. Foam tongue reshaped so its edges sit on the waterline.
- Build 6 (9 Oct 2026): breaking waves. A swell builds offshore, foam runs up the beach and pulls back, and whatever the wave carried is lying on the sand when it does. The next wave over an item has an even chance of taking it back. The fixed driftwood and kelp pickups are gone: everything scattered on the beach comes from the waves, and the beach's contents persist in the save (S.shore). Rocks and pilings only go wet, then sparkle, when a wave actually covers them. Brimble's driftwood directions updated. Fixed a double-draw of wash-ups from build 5.
- Build 5 (9 Oct 2026): the tide and Foraging. A real-time 24-minute tide shared by everyone: low water bares a wet band of sand that is walkable, the foam line and high-water mark move, the north rocks sit in their pools. Wash-ups ride in on the wave with a glint, rest on the wet sand for a window (longer with Foraging level), then the backwash takes them; Grab to forage driftwood, kelp, shells, bait or a rare wobblewood log. Hard surfaces the waves touch (rocks, dock pilings) go wet then sparkle for a moment; Scrape salt during the sparkle for a pinch of sea salt. Foraging subskill under Gathering with discoveries, achievements and unlocks; journal entries for the tide and Foraging. #dev exposes setTide, spawnWash, SURF, washes.
- Build 4 (9 Oct 2026): the skill tree. Five skills (Gathering, Survival, Invention, Exploration, Dominion), each a family of specific subskills; Hearth became Cooking and Firemaking, Shipwright became Woodcutting and Tinkering, saves migrate. Skills tab shows the five; tap one for its subskills (hidden ones show a clue), tap a subskill for its page. Skill XP sums subskills plus 50 per achievement; skill levels gate fires (Survival 3) and Kindle (Survival 6). Firemaking earns XP for building and feeding fires. Old Wick is now Brimble, galley flame of the Grinning Gull, with his own hollow in the north-east dunes (teapot, tripod pot, shelf). New icons: basket, crown, pan. Design plan for tide, Foraging, salt pans, Salt Tin, gull posts and Dominion written into docs/design-rules.md.
