# Tidecrown history (newest first)

- Build 31 (12 Oct 2026): dawn and dusk follow the sun. The light used to run on its own clock, so it was full daylight for most of an hour before the sun came up, and the only shadows then came from the setting moon, pointing east. Now it stays dark until a short twilight just before sunrise, the first shadows come with the sun and point west, and the moon only throws shadows in full dark. Dusk works the same way in reverse.
- Build 30 (12 Oct 2026): tapping no longer repeats lines. A tap does what it can, or walks you to the thing, and only says something you have not heard before; once a line has been seen, it stays quiet on later taps. Examine in the hold menu always says it, so anything can be reread. Lines about a full pack always show. Also: a quest log. The Journal opens on Quests (switch to Found for discoveries): each started quest shows who asked, why, and a checklist of steps with where to look in directions you can follow (the bone fid is on the reef off the south shore, a swim out past the shallows), and finished ones fold away. One quest is tracked: its next step sits at the top left (tap it to open the log) and its outstanding spots are marked on the map. The summit is a rounded dome now, so the shading no longer makes spokes, with a ring of bare rock and a stone cairn on top. Items you pick up fly from where they are drawn on the hill, not from below it. Ground no longer hides anything standing behind it (the camp rocks were being cut to slivers), and no tree grows beside Brimble's camp or in front of it, so the teapot and hearth always show.
- Build 29 (12 Oct 2026): fixes. Shadows no longer flip from side to side around noon: the sun now follows its real path for a tropical latitude, passing a little south of overhead, so noon shadows are short, point a little north and swing round gradually. Thought bubbles show up to three lines at a time before paging on. Clouds drift much more slowly, and the fog wisps that were racing about (they were tied to you and jumped with the wind) now sit in the world and drift gently, only where there is fog. The summit's cloud cap is sized to the larger island and sits over the summit. Wash-ups land only on the beach below the strand line, and any left inland by the island's growth have moved down to the beach. The edges between sand, scrub and grass curve and wander instead of meeting in sharp corners.
- Build 28 (12 Oct 2026): the Key is 67% larger and its summit about seven character heights up, with 30 wobble trees in the fog belt, 28 palms and more grass tufts. Fixes: the setting moon no longer throws shadows at dawn (they pointed east before sunrise and flipped west when the sun came up); a tap between trees walks you there instead of saying the hatchet line again (the line plays once, and on Examine), and things are hit only where they are drawn; thought bubbles sit above your head on hills, are a little see-through and show at most two lines at a time, paging on for longer thoughts, and the longest lines are shorter; Brimble now sits in a ring of fire stones with two flat hearthstones by the tripod, replacing the three loose pebbles, and the camp's rock outcrop sits behind the tripod; the shelf blends into the hill more gently, so its contour rings and the sliver-rocks are gone; edges between ground colours are softened.
- Build 27 (12 Oct 2026): the Key becomes a small high island. From the beach and a modest dune the land climbs steadily to a summit about four character heights up, steeper and rockier on the windward east, gentler on the lee west where the beaches are wide. Much more of the island is sand now, with palms growing on it, then a scrub belt and grassy lower slopes. The wobble trees grow only in a fog belt high on the hill, a small cloud forest kept wet by a cloud cap that sits on the summit most of the time, thickest in the morning; walking up into it is walking into fog. The very top is open rock. Brimble's camp sits on a level shelf on the upper lee shoulder just below the fog belt, the stone head on a smaller western shoulder, the still pool on a level spring-fed patch of the slope. The pier stays east. Key trees are all new positions, so any tree you had chopped is whole again.
- Build 26 (12 Oct 2026): Brimble's camp moves near the middle of the Key, onto a flat shelf on the lee side of a hill just below its crown, so he can keep watch for ships all round while the hillside, a low rock outcrop on the windward edge and a stand of trees on the north side shelter him from the wind and spray. The dune ring from build 25 is gone. His teapot now hangs on the tripod over the cooking spot in place of the cauldron. Every hold or right-click menu now offers Walk here. The Map tab is turned so north is up, like the game.

The ground is painted point by point with every point at its height, nearer ground covering what is behind it, and the coast, the grass edge and any crest that hides ground are traced from it as smooth ink, so the straight seams and the squared-off west shore from build 24 are gone, along with the wrong silhouette on your character. Things behind a ridge are cut off at it and you, creatures, people and items show a soft ink silhouette there. Brimble's teapot is 60% smaller. The surface builds in the first moments after loading, with the old flat drawing standing in until it is ready.

The Key's height is back up (the foredune about a character tall, the knoll about two), and where a dune face rises faster than the view, the ridge stands in front of the beach and water behind it. - Build 23 (12 Oct 2026): the land gets real dimension. Everything on high ground is drawn higher on screen by its height: you, the trees, rocks, the stone head, things on the ground, their shadows, the grass edge, the contours and the shading, so a dune's sea face stretches toward you and a hill stands up off the page. Heights are exaggerated to suit the world's scale, but never so much that a slope folds over itself, so nothing hides behind a ridge and taps land where you see them. The dune face is a little wider and gentler. The odd glow and smear at the waterline by the stone head is gone (the knoll now fades out across the beach instead of ending in a little cliff at the water). Contour lines are barely visible. Shading updates swap in all at once, so no seams appear while the light changes.
- Build 22 (12 Oct 2026): the ground reads as land instead of splotches. The Key's dunes are now real beach dunes: a flat wet beach, a foredune ridge along the shore with a steep sea face and a gentle back slope, a low swale behind it, then the grass, with the ridge rising and falling along the shore and dropping into a blowout in one place; the stone head sits on a smooth knoll and the step at the grass edge is gone. Shading is smooth and blended, worked out from the shape of the land rather than a coarse grid: a soft sky light leaves valleys and the foot of slopes slightly darker at any hour, the sun lights and shades the sides on top of that, hills cast long soft shadows at dawn and dusk, and higher ground is a shade warmer. Contour lines are fainter, with every fifth a little stronger, like a survey map. You lean into a climb and back going down, and sand slides off a dune face as you come down it.
- Build 21 (12 Oct 2026): fixes the flashing. Clouds were jumping thousands of pixels a frame (their position multiplied the wandering wind by the absolute clock), which also flickered every shadow; their drift now builds up smoothly. Clouds are now cumulus silhouettes with lobed tops and flat bases, drifting slowly with the wind, and they grow or shrink as cover changes instead of popping in. Shadows now behave like real ones: shadows from the same light no longer darken where they overlap, not trunk over canopy and not tree over tree, while sun, moon and firelight shadows still combine; under a cloud the sun shadows vanish and only a faint contact shadow stays; dense canopy darkens the ground beneath it. Shadows fall on the ground only.
- Build 20 (12 Oct 2026): shadow tree harvest and regrowth (test build until the Shadow Forest opens). Every shadow tree has branches, limbs and a trunk, and Woodcutting level sets how deep you can go: branches from any tree, limbs and felling at each wood's level, so a beginner can still take ironbark branches instead of bouncing off. Each harvest also brings down some of the layers below it, and a branches-only harvest gives the most branches. The tree keeps what you left: branches grow back in about an hour or more, limbs in hours, and a felled tree comes back over about a day (black wood), two (twisted) or three (ironbark), as stump, sapling, young tree, then full, with its shade returning as it grows. Stumps keep the size the tree had reached. Dig one out for root wood and a chance of fatwood, a burl, grubs for bait or, rarely, a rotted pouch of shells; the spot sits bare a while longer before a sapling shows. Saplings give a straight pole. Forests now start with saplings, young trees and dead snags among the grown trees. New items: branches and limbs for each wood, pole, root wood, fatwood, burl. Trees felled in older saves come back as fresh stumps.
- Build 19 (12 Oct 2026): terrain height. The Key has low dunes along the sand and one low rise inland, with the stone head on top. In the test build the dark islands each get a landform: rolling hills on the Thicket, ravines through the Tangle, a tall lookout hill on the Far Grove, a shallow bowl for the Clearing, a sunken basin for the Fallow, and the Hollow in a real hollow. Soft contour lines and sun hillshade show the ground, with slopes facing away from the sun in shade that moves through the day. Walking slows uphill and on steep descents and is quickest on a gentle downhill (Tobler's hiking function). Fog settles in low ground and thins on high ground, where you also see farther and the view eases out a little. Nothing is impassable yet.
- Build 18 (12 Oct 2026): weather, the same for everyone because it runs off the clock. A trade wind from the east wanders in direction and strength; trees and palms sway with it, the scarf streams, cooking smoke drifts, rain slants. Waves still reach every shore, but the big ones now come from the windward side. Clouds travel with the wind and their shade slides over the ground; under one, shadows go faint. Once in a while a low cloud drifts below the view as soft white. Squalls blow through about one quarter hour in seven: the light greys, small fires drown in seconds, big ones burn down faster, Brimble's hearth holds, and drying salt washes off. Dawn fog comes some mornings and burns off by late morning, clearing fastest on the sand; the cold water over the current keeps a sea fog. Fog shortens how far you see, and anything beyond your sight cannot be tapped. The test build's World section gets weather (auto, clear, cloudy, fog, squall) and a wind turn.
- Build 17 (12 Oct 2026): light and day. The sky alone sets the light: the Shadow Forest no longer sits under a fixed darkness at every hour, and the sky no longer dims as you swim out (the water still darkens). A tropical sun rises east, passes nearly overhead and sets west, and everything casts a shadow away from it: trees and palms (canopy and trunk), rocks, the pier, NPCs, creatures, you and things on the ground; long west in the morning, nearly none at noon, long east in the evening. A moon on the opposite path casts faint shadows at night, and every lit fire casts shadows outward, stronger after dark. Shadow trees cast deep canopy shade that overlaps where the forest is dense, with a few coins of sunlight through the gaps, so the darkness of each island now comes from its trees. A chopped Key tree casts a thinner canopy shadow until its leaves grow back.

- Build 14 (9 Oct 2026): the scarf. Every character wears one and it is the weapon, in the colour picked at the still pool (the You tab's Scarf slot shows it and the controls). Four moves, two skills: tap a thing in reach to slap it and slide at it to trip it (Strife); slide from it toward you to wrap it, and slide back again while it is wrapped to bind it (Accord). Hold on a thing in reach and the menu lists the moves too. Dominion and Strife unlock on the first slap, Accord on the first wrap. Reach starts short and grows with Dominion level (new file 26-scarf). Three slaps in rhythm is a Flurry burst. Hollowmaws: a slap or trip on a still one wakes it (the chest creaks, then the lunge), so slapping dead trees tells the real ones apart; a slap on the creak counters it and it stops; a trip on the creak puts it down (Driven off burst) and a limb comes off as dead wood, a new item that burns in your fires; a wrap on the creak mirrors it and it holds still, calm, chest open; bind it then with an ember pearl in your pack and it plants, becoming a young tree with a warm light in it, saved for good (Planted burst and journal). Mossbacks: they shiver now and then, their tell; a wrap on the shiver warms one and it follows for a couple of minutes (Mirrored); bind a wrapped one and it is yours (Kept); slap or trip one and it squeaks and runs. Old Bollard, an iron mooring post with a face at the root of the pier, is the practice post on the Key: he leans toward you now and then; trip or slap him on the lean to ring him (Rung), wrap him on the lean and he is happiest; his XP fades the more you practise. Parent skills now run 1 to 10 on their own scale (PXP): level 2 at 100 XP and each level after needs about 1.62 times the one before (doubling every 1.43 levels), so level 10 sits at about 4800; a skill's XP is 5% of its subskills' XP plus 50 per achievement, so it always trails them and 10 comes when the subskills are deep in the 60s and 70s; subskills keep 1 to 99.
